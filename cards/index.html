2026 AFL finals: match cards

Every final as a match card: in-game win probability around the outside, every shot's expected score (xS) on the ground, replayable event by event.

Matches

Reading a match card

  • Ring: win probability of the team in front, 50% at the inner edge to 100% at the outer, in its colour. Clockwise from 12 o'clock, one quarter per quadrant.
  • Ticks round the ring: each shot at its time; length and label are its xS. ● goal, ○ behind, × no score.
  • Ground: where each shot came from, circle area = xS. Every venue is drawn as Marvel Stadium, true to scale inside 50 m.
  • Left panels: score, team xS, inside 50s, the top 5 players on a stat you pick, and how each team's xS was created.
  • Interact: press Play or scrub; hover a shot for the possession chain behind it; Key explains every mark.

Win probability model

A logistic GAM of the home team's chance of winning, refreshed at every event. Four inputs, each with a weight that changes smoothly as the match goes on: bookmaker pre-game odds, the margin, the expected points of the current possession, and the xS difference this quarter. Margin and possession value are scaled by √(time left), so the same lead counts for more late.

Expected score (xS) model

Two logistic stages: does the shot score, then is it a goal; xS = 6 × P(goal) + P(behind). Inputs: location (distance, angle and a location surface, separately for set shots and open play), shot type, the action before the shot, time since the previous possession, ball speed, game state, weather, roof, position and venue. Each player gets correlated set-shot and open-play finishing effects (Bayesian, partially pooled); the cards use an average finisher.
